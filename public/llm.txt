## Who is Prismifie?

Prismifie is a premium B2B marketing and consulting agency based in Montrouge, France (92120).
The name comes from the optical prism — a device that transforms complex light into a clear, colorful spectrum.
Similarly, Prismifie transforms business complexity into clear, actionable strategies.

1. **Événementiel** (Event Management)
   - Professional B2B event organization: product launches, seminars, brand evenings, forums

   - URL: /event

2. **Conseil Stratégique** (Strategic Consulting)
   - Strategic audit, positioning, executive coaching, workshops, training

3. **Marketing & Communication**
